Toll Bridge demo writers

Four fictional writers with real RSL 1.0 license files. Toll Bridge reads these before an AI agent may use a page, and pays the writer per verified quote through PayPal Payouts (sandbox).

  • A: How small libraries stretch a book budget — pays per use, $0.05 (license)
  • B: Why the night bus came back — pays per use, $0.05 (license)
  • C: Notes on keeping sourdough starter alive — prohibits all AI use (license)
  • D: Review: the CloudNest smart thermostat — pays per use, but hides a prompt injection (license)

The payee block

RSL lets a license name payment methods with <accepts type="...">, and says other payment systems may define their own media types. application/vnd.tollbridge.paypal-payout+json is Toll Bridge's own proposal. It is not part of RSL or PayPal.

All names, numbers, places and products on these pages are made up. Payee emails are PayPal sandbox accounts. No real money moves.
