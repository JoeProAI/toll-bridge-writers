Notes on keeping sourdough starter alive

By June Okonkwo (fictional demo writer)

I have kept the same starter going for eleven years. I feed it once a day at room temperature, at a ratio of one part starter to five parts flour and five parts water by weight.

When I travel, it goes in the fridge, where it is fine for up to three weeks without feeding. After that, it needs two or three feedings at room temperature before it rises reliably again.
